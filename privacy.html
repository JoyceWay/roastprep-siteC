Privacy

Short version: we do not collect anything about you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no advertising and no analytics. The pages do not load anything from other companies.

Where progress is kept

Which episodes you have finished, your guesses and your stickers are saved in your own browser, on your own device, using the browser’s local storage. This never leaves the device. Clearing the browser’s site data erases it.

Who delivers the pages

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare’s servers receive the technical details needed to send a page, such as the IP address of the device asking for it. We do not look at, keep or combine that information.

Children

RoastPrep is written for students in about grades 7 to 11. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Links to other sites

Each episode lists its sources. Those links lead to other websites, which have their own privacy practices.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before it does. Last updated: November 23, 2026.
